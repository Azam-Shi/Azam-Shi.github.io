About Me

I am a PhD candidate at Florida International University's Knight Foundation School of Computing and Information Sciences, working in the BioRG Research Group under the supervision of Prof. Giri Narasimhan. My dissertation focuses on AI-driven approaches in structural biology — from modeling biomolecular interactions to designing novel proteins using generative AI.

I am deeply passionate about harnessing the potential of deep learning and generative AI to unlock new possibilities in structural biology, particularly in protein modeling and design. My work focuses on utilizing breakthrough technologies to address the complexities of protein design and protein-protein interactions, which are crucial for developing innovative therapeutics and understanding the molecular foundations of life. I thrive on the challenge of exploring cutting-edge approaches to reveal the intricate beauty of proteins and their transformative roles in our health. Collaboration and teamwork are at the heart of my approach. I believe in the power of collective curiosity, working with like-minded individuals to solve problems, share discoveries, and make meaningful contributions to bioinformatics.
